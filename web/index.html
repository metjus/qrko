Static QR codes with no service expiry

This tool creates static QR codes: the encoded content cannot be changed after generation. The generator does not set an expiry date. A linked website still needs to remain available, and changed Wi-Fi or contact details require a new code.

Frequently asked questions

Short answers to the questions people ask most often.

Do you store my data or the contents of the QR code?

No. Everything is generated in your browser using JavaScript. The text, link or Wi-Fi password you enter is never sent to a server and is not stored anywhere.

How do I scan the QR code I created?

Open the camera app on your phone and point it at the code. Most modern phones, both Android and iPhone, recognise it automatically without any extra app and show the link for confirmation.

Is the generator free to use?

Yes, it is free and requires no account. You are welcome to use the codes you create commercially.

Do these QR codes expire?

The generator does not set an expiry date on static QR codes. They remain usable while the code is readable and the encoded information is still valid. A web link must stay available; changed Wi-Fi or contact details require a new code.

What quality is the downloaded code?

The code downloads as a 600 × 600 pixel PNG. That is enough for business cards, flyers and smaller posters. For large-format printing, leave clear white space around the code so scanners read it reliably.
