Cookie Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

This English text is a translation provided for convenience. In case of any discrepancy, the Slovak version of this document prevails.

This site respects your privacy and keeps its use of cookies to the strict minimum. Below is an overview of the technologies it uses.

What cookies are

Cookies are small text files a website stores in your browser. A similar technology, localStorage, is used here to keep your settings on your own device.

What we use

cookie_consent_statusEssential (localStorage)Remembers your cookie consent choiceUntil browser data is cleared

Cookieless traffic measurement

For traffic figures we use Cloudflare Web Analytics. It stores no cookies or other identifiers on your device and does not track you across websites, so it does not depend on your consent. It collects only aggregate data on page views, country and browser type.

Advertising cookies

This site uses Adsterra advertising cookies (Native Banner and Social Bar formats) to display ads. These cookies are set, and the related scripts loaded, only after you consent through the cookie bar, and never on the Contact page.

Until you consent, no third-party advertising scripts run at all.

Managing cookies

You can change your choice at any time by clearing your browser data, after which the cookie bar appears again. You can also manage cookies and local storage directly in your browser settings (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge).
